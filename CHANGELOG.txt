AION 2 CHARACTER TRACKER - CHANGELOG
====================================
All data is stored only in each person's own browser (localStorage).
Updating the site never resets anyone's saved characters.


2026-11-06
----------
- New "Party Planner" page in the sidebar: two parties of five (Tank, DPS,
  DPS, DPS/C, SUP) plus a bench. Drag a player onto another slot to swap
  (tap one, then tap the target, on phones), shuffle everyone, add / edit /
  delete players, and copy the lineup as text. Class colours: Templar dark
  blue, Gladiator light blue, Chanter orange. Saved in your browser only.
- Party Planner: new "Find a character" search. Type a name, pick your region
  and server, and it fetches the class and combat power from the official
  character search and adds the character to the bench (or updates them if
  they are already in the planner).
- Party Planner: now starts empty (the sample players are gone) and Reset
  clears the whole page, including the bench and the search box. Reset is red
  and asks you to click twice to confirm. All Planner buttons now have emojis.
- Party Planner: the role on the left of each party slot (Tank, DPS, DPS/C, SUP)
  is now a dropdown you can change. Roles belong to the slot and are saved;
  Reset puts them back to the defaults.
- Party Planner: Sorcerer is now violet and Spirit Master orchid purple (colours
  taken from the in-game icons), and the role dropdowns are wider so they are
  no longer squished.
- Party Planner: Ranger is a slightly darker green, and Assassin now has its own
  colour, a slightly brighter green than Ranger.
- Party Planner fix: Spiritmaster now gets its colour (the class name is spelled
  "Spiritmaster" in the game lookup, which did not match the colour list).
  Class colours now ignore spacing and capital letters.
- Party Planner: each party now shows its average combat power next to the
  total (players with an unknown power are left out of the average).
- Party Planner: new "Refresh combat power" button. It re-checks every player
  added through the search (and any player whose name matches a character in
  your Character Tracker) and updates their combat power. Players added by
  hand are skipped because the game lookup needs their region and server.
